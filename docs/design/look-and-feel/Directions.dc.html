1 — Bande dessinée
The UI is a comic: ink panels, bubbles, hand-lettering, halftone, sound-effect words.
Funniest by construction; the party's voice is on every screen.
Ink can get loud in combat — panels there must stay calm. Needs consistent line-art from the image generator.
2 — Torchlit corridor
The dungeon is the atmosphere; the chrome is modern and quiet. Warm light, depth, vignette.
Closest to STS2's register; easiest to keep dynamic without clutter; forgiving of mixed AI art styles.
The humour has to come from portraits, copy and animation — the chrome carries none.
3 — Adventurer's notebook
Everything is drawn into the party's logbook: scrawled map, pinned cards, margin notes in different hands.
Most tactile and characterful; margin notes are a free banter channel.
Parchment-fantasy cliché unless the doodles carry real jokes; hardest to keep legible at density.
4 — Saturday-morning flat
Bold flat vectors, thick outlines, limited saturated palette, springy motion.
Cheapest to produce at quality; most obviously modern and dynamic; strong silhouettes.
Least dungeon-y — the palette and backdrops must do the atmosphere work.
